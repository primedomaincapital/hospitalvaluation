## market-data.html — figure sources (all figures seen in public page text)

- Kaufman Hall 2025 year-end M&A report, via Healthcare Dive (January 20, 2026): 46 transactions announced in 2025, down from 72 in 2024, lowest since tracking began in 2011; $18.5 billion transacted revenue, lowest since 2018; 43.5% of deals involved a financially distressed party (15% in 2022, 27.7% in 2023, 30.6% in 2024); average distressed-deal smaller-party revenue $345 million. https://www.healthcaredive.com/news/hospital-health-system-mergers-acquisitions-decline-2025-policy-uncertainty-kaufman-hall/809916/
- Fierce Healthcare (January 16, 2026): 2024 transacted revenue $39.7 billion; Anu Singh (Kaufman Hall) on the distress trend continuing into 2026. https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/providers/uncertainty-financial-distress-dominated-hospital-ma-2025
- HealthLeaders Media (January 2026): 46 deals in 2025; $18.5 billion transacted revenue; 43.5% distressed. https://www.healthleadersmedia.com/ceo/hospital-ma-slumped-2025-financial-distress-policy-uncertainty-stalled-deals
- Becker's Hospital Review (January 15, 2026): distressed share progression 15% / 27.7% / 30.6% / 43.5%. https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/hospital-transactions-and-valuation/why-hospital-ma-slowed-in-2025-per-kaufman-hall/
- Kaufman Hall M&A Quarterly Activity Report Q1 2026, via Becker's Hospital Review (April 9, 2026) and Health Capital Topics (April 2026): 22 deals; $14.5 billion transacted revenue; average seller $657 million (second-highest since 2018); 3 mega mergers; Sutter Health ($20B revenue) and Allina Health ($6B revenue) announced March 17, 2026, combined $26 billion; 15 of 22 deals (68%) divestitures versus 45.6% in 2025; only 4 of 22 (18%) involved a distressed seller. https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/hospital-transactions-and-valuation/22-deals-14-5b-transacted-revenue-what-robust-q1-hospital-ma-signals/ and https://www.healthcapital.com/hcc/newsletter/04_26/HTML/HOSPITAL/hospital_ma_q1_2026.php
- Kaufman Hall Q2 2026, via Healthcare Dive (July 13, 2026): 18 deals; $7.7 billion transacted revenue versus 8 deals and $1.4 billion in Q2 2025; only 3 of 18 involved a distressed seller. https://www.healthcaredive.com/news/hospital-health-system-mergers-acquisitions-momentum-kaufman-hall-q2-2026/825114/
- Kaufman Hall Q3 2026, via Becker's Hospital Review and Fierce Healthcare (October 14, 2026): 16 deals; $8.2 billion transacted revenue; 56 deals through Q3 2026 versus 46 in all of 2025; average seller $586 million (Q3 2025: $401 million); two mega mergers (Prisma Health–Erlanger; HealthPartners–Essentia, above $12 billion combined); Rush–Endeavor 12 hospitals, nearly $11 billion, $1 billion investment pledge. https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/hospital-transactions-and-valuation/hospital-ma-on-pace-for-busiest-year-since-pandemic-as-megamergers-mount/ and https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/providers/big-ticket-hospital-deals-are-becoming-ma-story-2026
- Kaufman Hall National Hospital Flash Report, August 2026, via Becker's Hospital Review (October 22, 2026): median operating margin 1.8% in August (3.8% July, 5.6% June), calendar-year-to-date 2.5%; roughly 43% of hospitals operating at a loss, up from about 33% pre-pandemic; bad debt and charity care 13%; data from more than 1,300 hospitals. https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/finance/why-43-of-hospitals-are-losing-money/
- American Hospital Association, Fast Facts on U.S. Hospitals, 2026 (updated February 2026): 6,100 hospitals; 2024 survey data from more than 6,200 hospitals and 400 health systems. 2025 edition: 6,093 hospitals. https://www.aha.org/statistics/fast-facts-us-hospitals
- Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health hospital-closure study via Advisory Board (October 13, 2026): 432 hospitals closed and 216 opened 2010–2025; net −216 hospitals and −24,399 beds; closure rate up about 4% a year, openings down about 3%. https://www.advisory.com/daily-briefing/2026/10/13/hospital-closures-oi-ec
- FOCUS Investment Banking, Healthcare EBITDA Multiples: 2026 Dashboard: hospitals 6.3x EBITDA ($1–3M), 8.2x ($3–5M), 9.7x ($5–10M); revenue multiples 1.0x ($1–5M), 1.5x ($6–10M), 2.0x ($10–50M); public median EV/EBITDA about 11.5x in 2026 versus 14.5x the prior year. https://focusib.com/insights/article/healthcare-ebitda-multiples/
